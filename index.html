📜 Chapter V of V • The Royal Keepsake Finale

Shubh Deepavali, Mehak!

“तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय” • May Light Always Guide Your Path

✦ A Royal Deepavali Scroll ✦

Dearest Mehak,

Flipping through the chapters of life, some people are like gentle Diyas—they don’t just shine for themselves, they quietly make the world around them warmer, brighter, and infinitely more beautiful. You are that rare light, Mehak.

As thousands of lamps flicker against the October night sky, I wanted to craft this little palace of light to remind you how deeply appreciated and cherished you are. Thank you for the effortless grace, the genuine laughter, and the wonderful positivity you bring into every moment.

"हर दिया आपकी मुस्कुराहट सा जगमगाए,
हर खुशी आपके आँगन में फूलों सी खिल जाए,
इस दिवाली लक्ष्मी जी और खुशियों का ऐसा आशीर्वाद मिले,
कि आपका हर सपना सोने सा सच हो जाए!" ✨

May this Festival of Lights mark the beginning of your happiest, healthiest, and most triumphant chapter yet. Keep shining your unstoppable light!

With Warmest Wishes & Gratitude,

Rohit Rathi 🪔✨
